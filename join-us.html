FOUR ACADEMIC PATHWAYS

Bring your subject knowledge.
Find where it can contribute.

We are inviting students from four programmes offered by the Department of Professional Studies. These pathways show possible ways to contribute — not limits on what a student can explore.

01 / MANAGEMENT & STRATEGYGMAS

B.Com Global Management Accounting & Strategy

Integrated with CIMA UK

Management accounting and strategy concepts can help students examine how a business uses its resources, monitors performance and makes operating decisions.

Ways you could contribute

  • Cost and expense analysis
  • Budgeting support
  • Business performance tracking
  • Pricing and margin research
  • Operational efficiency ideas

Business contribution: Help owners understand costs, performance and practical opportunities to use resources more effectively.

02 / ACCOUNTANCY & FINANCEIAF

B.Com International Accountancy & Finance

Integrated with ACCA

Accounting and finance concepts can help students interpret business information, understand financial performance and explore stronger financial practices.

Ways you could contribute

  • Financial information analysis
  • Working-capital research
  • Revenue trend analysis
  • Financial reporting support
  • Risk and control awareness

Business contribution: Help businesses make more informed decisions using organised financial information and clear analysis.

03 / BRANDING & ENTREPRENEURSHIPB&E

B.Com Branding & Entrepreneurship

Programme focus: Brand building & enterprise

Branding and entrepreneurial thinking can help students understand customers, shape a business identity and explore new ways to reach the market.

Ways you could contribute

  • Brand positioning
  • Customer and market research
  • Digital marketing ideas
  • Customer acquisition plans
  • New business opportunities

Business contribution: Help businesses communicate their value, connect with customers and identify opportunities to develop.

04 / ACCOUNTANCY & FINANCEA&F

B.Com Accountancy & Finance

Integrated with CA – ICAI

Accounting and taxation concepts can help students understand the importance of accurate records, sound documentation and responsible financial processes.

Ways you could contribute

  • Bookkeeping process research
  • Financial record organisation
  • Taxation research support
  • Audit documentation awareness
  • Internal-control observations

Business contribution: Help businesses work towards better organised records and more reliable financial information.

Students are selected on interest, aptitude and readiness to learn. Programme integration does not mean every student has completed or mastered the full professional qualification syllabus. Any specialist or regulated work will remain subject to appropriate competence, supervision and professional requirements.

A RESPONSIBLE WAY TO WORK

From a business question
to a considered next step.

We want projects to be useful to businesses and meaningful for students. That starts with listening, agreeing boundaries and reviewing what changed.

01

Understand

Listen to the owner, learn how the business works and clarify the challenge they want to explore.

02

Research

Gather relevant information, ask informed questions and work within the team's skills and available evidence.

03

Develop

Prepare practical options, agree the scope and obtain approval before any implementation begins.

04

Review

Document the work, discuss outcomes and reflect on what the team and business learned.
