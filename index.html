A NEIGHBOURHOOD WITH MANY CHAPTERS

Rooted in S.G. Palya.
Built for what’s next.

BENGALOOP begins with one simple idea: local discovery should feel as easy as asking someone who knows the neighbourhood. We are starting with S.G. Palya — its shops, food spots, homes, services and the people who make it a community.

Our aim is to make nearby places easier to find while giving neighbourhood businesses a simple way to be seen online.

A little about the neighbourhood’s past ↗
A FEW CHAPTERS IN THE STORYS.G. PALYA · BENGALURU
AROUND 1975

A quieter edge of the city

A resident quoted by The New Indian Express recalled S.G. Palya as a relatively open area, with Hosur Road visible from home. This is a personal recollection, not a complete historical record.

THE 1990s

A neighbourhood shaped by weaving

Later reporting describes Suddaguntepalya as one of Bengaluru’s weaving strongholds, with handlooms and power looms forming part of everyday life.

TODAY

A lively, mixed neighbourhood

Students, residents, workers, PGs, food spots, shops and everyday services now share these streets. The area continues to evolve.

2026 · THE NEXT CHAPTER

BENGALOOP joins the neighbourhood

We’re building a simple way to discover local businesses and services, starting with S.G. Palya.

Naval Aggarwal Founder & CEO

A B.Com student in Global Management Accounting at CHRIST (Deemed to be University), Bengaluru, Naval initiated BENGALOOP to make it easier for people to discover neighbourhood businesses, accommodation and everyday services while helping local businesses become more visible. As Founder & CEO, he leads the venture's strategy and planning, oversees the website and technology experience, and uses research and analytics to guide product decisions and growth.
