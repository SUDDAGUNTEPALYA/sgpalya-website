THE BENGALOOP MODEL

One neighbourhood.
A better-connected local market.

Local information is scattered. People miss useful places nearby, while smaller businesses can struggle to be discovered. BENGALOOP brings people, businesses and community connections into one local loop.

THE PRINCIPLE WE WILL NOT COMPROMISE

Small businesses should not have to become big to deserve visibility.

Every business should have a fair opportunity to be found. Basic directory access will remain distinct from paid promotions. If sponsored placements are introduced later, they will be clearly labelled.

Naval Aggarwal Founder & CEO

A B.Com student in Global Management Accounting at CHRIST (Deemed to be University), Bengaluru, Naval initiated BENGALOOP to make local discovery easier, create fairer visibility for businesses of every size and bring a scattered neighbourhood market into one connected platform. As Founder & CEO, he leads the venture's strategy and platform development while building its future across business marketing support and community-led growth.
