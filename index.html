S.G. PALYA is a neighbourhood discovery initiative built to connect people with the businesses, accommodation and everyday services around S.G. Palya, Bengaluru.

Whether you live here, study nearby, work in the area or are just visiting, we want to make finding what you need a little easier — while helping local businesses get noticed.

Your Neighbourhood. Everything Connected.

From looms to a lively local hub.

S.G. Palya's story is one of change: a weaving neighbourhood transformed by Bengaluru's growth into a busy, mixed community of residents, students, workers and local businesses.

BEFORE THE BOOM

A quieter edge of the city

A resident quoted by The New Indian Express recalled living in S.G. Palya around 1975, when the area was still relatively open and Hosur Road could be seen from his home. This is a personal recollection rather than a complete record of the locality.

1990s

A neighbourhood shaped by weaving

Later reporting describes Suddaguntepalya as one of Bengaluru's weaving strongholds in the 1990s, with handlooms and power looms forming part of everyday life. The craft remains part of the area's living memory, even as the number of working loom units has fallen.

FROM THE MID-1990s

Urban growth changes the streets

A 2010 report connected faster development in the area with the growth of nearby technology employment from the mid-1990s. Flats, rented rooms and businesses increasingly served professionals and students.

TODAY

A busy, mixed neighbourhood

Today, S.G. Palya is widely associated with student life, PGs and hostels, food spots, shops and everyday services, alongside long-time residents and traditional businesses. Its identity continues to evolve.

2026 · THE NEXT CHAPTER

S.G. PALYA begins connecting the neighbourhood

We started S.G. PALYA to make local discovery easier: helping people find nearby businesses and services, while giving neighbourhood enterprises a simple way to be seen online. We begin with S.G. Palya and intend to grow with the community.

NAVAL AGGARWAL FOUNDER & CEO

Building local connection,
one neighbourhood at a time.

Naval Aggarwal Founder & CEO

Naval Aggarwal is the Founder and CEO of S.G. PALYA, a neighbourhood discovery initiative based in Bengaluru. He is pursuing a B.Com in Global Management Accounting at CHRIST (Deemed to be University), Bengaluru.

With a commerce student's perspective on business, markets and how organisations create value, Naval is interested in using simple digital tools to solve practical, everyday problems. S.G. PALYA grew from a straightforward observation: people who live, study or work around the neighbourhood often need an easier way to discover local businesses, accommodation and essential services, while many small businesses need better visibility among the people already around them.

“I want S.G. PALYA to make the neighbourhood easier to navigate and its local businesses easier to discover. We are starting small, listening to the community and building something useful, one local connection at a time.”
