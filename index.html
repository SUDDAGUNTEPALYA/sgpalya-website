BENGALOOP is being built to make local commerce more organised, inclusive and connected. Today, people often rely on scattered information or word of mouth to find nearby services, while smaller businesses can struggle to reach people who are already close by.

A FEW CHAPTERS IN THE STORYS.G. PALYA · BENGALURU
AROUND 1975

A quieter edge of the city

A resident quoted by The New Indian Express recalled S.G. Palya as a relatively open area, with Hosur Road visible from home. This is a personal recollection, not a complete historical record.

THE 1990s

A neighbourhood shaped by weaving

Later reporting describes Suddaguntepalya as one of Bengaluru’s weaving strongholds, with handlooms and power looms forming part of everyday life.

TODAY

A lively, mixed neighbourhood

Students, residents, workers, PGs, food spots, shops and everyday services now share these streets. The area continues to evolve.

2026 · THE NEXT CHAPTER

BENGALOOP joins the neighbourhood

We’re building a simple way to discover local businesses and services, starting with S.G. Palya.

GOOD TO KNOW

A few things worth knowing.

Clear answers about what BENGALOOP does today and how the neighbourhood directory works.

What is BENGALOOP?

BENGALOOP is a neighbourhood discovery platform that helps people find local businesses, accommodation and everyday services around S.G. Palya, Bengaluru.

What can I discover here?

You can explore food and drink, shopping, healthcare, PGs and housing, education and everyday local services. Availability depends on the approved public listings currently in the directory.

Does BENGALOOP cover all of Bengaluru?

The platform is starting in S.G. Palya. Its current focus is making this neighbourhood easier to navigate before expanding its local coverage.

How do listings appear in the directory?

The public directory displays entries marked as approved. A listing is not shown simply because information has been submitted to an internal or private source.

How can I contact a listed place?

Use the contact options shown on an individual listing. Phone, WhatsApp, website and map links appear only when the relevant details are available.

Can I register my business on the website right now?

The business registration screen is currently a preview. It does not submit or save information, so the live registration workflow is not open yet.

Will paid promotions affect ordinary listings?

BENGALOOP's stated principle is to keep basic directory access distinct from paid promotions. Any sponsored placements introduced in the future will be clearly labelled.
