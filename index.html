THE PEOPLE BEHIND THE LOOP

Building local connection,
one neighbourhood at a time.

Naval Aggarwal Founder & CEO

A B.Com student in Global Management Accounting at CHRIST (Deemed to be University), Bengaluru, Naval initiated BENGALOOP to make local discovery easier, create fairer visibility for businesses of every size and bring a scattered neighbourhood market into one connected platform. As Founder & CEO, he leads the venture's strategy and platform development while building its future across business marketing support and community-led growth.
