THE PRINCIPLE WE WILL NOT COMPROMISE

Small businesses should not have to become big to deserve visibility.

Every business should have a fair opportunity to be found. Basic directory access will remain distinct from paid promotions. If sponsored placements are introduced later, they will be clearly labelled.

THE BIGGER PICTURE

More than a directory.
A connected local ecosystem.

BENGALOOP starts by making neighbourhood information easier to find. The bigger opportunity is to connect the people who need local information, the businesses that provide it and the community networks that help it travel.
