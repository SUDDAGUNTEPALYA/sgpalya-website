THE PURPOSE

Protection begins
with understanding.

A business is built through years of effort. A single unexpected event can put its premises, stock, equipment, income or people under pressure.

Vyapar Kavach is designed to make business insurance easier to understand for small business owners. Through local conversations, one-to-one learning support and specialist-led workshops, we want owners to become aware of the risks they face, the kinds of protection available, and the questions worth asking before choosing a policy.

Our role is to make the subject clearer—not to push a product or make the decision on the owner's behalf.

WHAT BUSINESS OWNERS CAN EXPLORE01 — AWARENESS

Know what could
put business at risk.

Every business is different. These are examples of exposures to discuss—not a promise that any particular policy will cover them.

01

Property & fire

Premises, fixtures, furniture, stock and damage caused by insured events.

02

Theft & burglary

Loss of stock, cash or equipment, subject to policy definitions and safeguards.

03

Equipment & machinery

Breakdown, accidental damage and the cost of restoring essential equipment.

04

Transit & deliveries

Goods moving between suppliers, premises, customers and destinations.

05

Business interruption

Possible income disruption and continuing expenses after an insured event.

06

Liability & people

Third-party responsibilities, employee-related exposures and workplace risks.

Actual coverage depends on the wording, insured events, limits, exclusions, conditions and underwriting decision in the policy issued by the insurer.

THE VYAPAR KAVACH APPROACH02 — OUR THREE STAGES

From first conversation
to informed decisions.

A simple three-stage pathway designed around the time, needs and questions of local business owners.

STAGE0101 / 03
COMMUNITY OUTREACH & AWARENESS

Start with a conversation.

Vyapar Kavach project volunteers will visit small businesses in the starting neighbourhoods, introduce the basics of business insurance, and explain why understanding risk matters.

  • Meet business owners where they work.
  • Explain common business risks and basic insurance concepts in clear language.
  • Answer general awareness questions and share learning resources.
  • Only businesses that express interest and agree to follow-up will be added to an enquiry list.
Awareness first.

No pressure, no obligation to buy, and no assumption that every business needs the same cover.

STAGE0202 / 03
PERSONALISED LEARNING & COMPARISON SUPPORT

Understand what fits your business.

Businesses requesting further help will receive individualised educational support to clarify their exposures, understand relevant types of cover and prepare for an informed discussion with an authorised insurance professional.

  • Understand the business, its assets, activities and key concerns.
  • Learn the differences between relevant insurance categories and policy structures.
  • Read and compare available policy information using consistent criteria—not brand preference.
  • Prepare questions for a licensed insurance intermediary or insurer before any product recommendation or purchase.
⌕
Clarity before choice.

Policy-specific recommendations, quotations and placement are handled by appropriately authorised professionals—not project volunteers.

MERCHANT INSURANCE AWARENESS PROGRAMME

  • Interactive merchant awareness workshops.
  • Sessions with professionals across relevant insurance specialisations.
  • Practical discussions on policy language, risk prevention and claims processes.
  • Focused events shaped by the questions and needs raised by local businesses.

THE PRINCIPLE AT THE HEART OF THIS PROJECT

An informed choice.
Not a pushed product.

One of Vyapar Kavach's central aims is to help business owners approach insurance selection without pressure or hidden preference. A policy should be considered against the risks and needs of the business—not simply because a brand is familiar or a premium looks low.

01No insurer preferred by default.
02Compare policy terms, not just brand names or price.
03Disclose any referral, sponsorship or commercial relationship before an introduction.
04The business owner makes the final decision, without pressure.
A MORE USEFUL WAY TO COMPARE

Look beyond
the premium.

When policy information is available, owners can be supported to look at comparable details side by side and ask what each term means for their own business.

01Scope of cover

What property, activity, event or liability is actually insured?

02Limits & sub-limits

What is the maximum payable, and are any categories capped?

03Exclusions & conditions

What is not covered, and what safeguards or duties apply?

04Deductibles & excess

What part of a loss would the business need to bear?

05Premium & add-ons

What is the total cost, and what does each optional benefit add?

06Claims process

How must a loss be reported, documented and followed up?

A CLEAR AND RESPONSIBLE ROLE

Education, not
insurance distribution.

Vyapar Kavach is an insurance-awareness and learning initiative. Its volunteers are not presented as insurance agents, brokers, insurers or licensed insurance advisers. The project does not issue policies, bind cover, collect premiums, promise claim outcomes or make underwriting decisions.

Where an owner wants a product-specific recommendation, quotation or purchase, that step must be taken with an appropriately authorised insurance professional or insurer. Any referral or commercial relationship connected with an introduction must be disclosed clearly. Owners should review the insurer's final policy wording and proposal documents before deciding.
