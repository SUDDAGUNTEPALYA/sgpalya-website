09 / BUILT ON CLARITY

Good partnerships
need good boundaries.

01

Free to participate

No commissions, paid listings or premium access. MELD's current focus is building participation, trust and meaningful partnerships.

02

Choice on both sides

Businesses choose which opportunities to consider. Organisers choose which proposals to pursue. Either side can decline.

03

Clear, agreed benefits

Brand visibility, stalls, product sampling and other deliverables must be agreed in advance and allowed by the event venue or institution.

04

Respect for people and data

Contact details are shared only with permission. MELD does not promise access to student data, guaranteed sales or audience conversion.

10 / QUICK CLARITY

A few things
worth knowing.

Does MELD organise events or sell tickets? +

No. MELD focuses on sponsorship discovery, relevant introductions and partnership facilitation. Event planning, public event listings, registrations, ticketing and student community features are outside its scope.

Does MELD guarantee a sponsor? +

No. MELD works to identify relevant potential partners and facilitate introductions. Businesses make their own decisions, and organisers are responsible for agreeing the final sponsorship terms.

Can a business support an event without paying cash? +

Yes. Businesses may express interest in product, service, venue, prize or experience-based support, depending on the event requirement and the business's own preferences.

Is MELD an official CHRIST University platform? +

No. MELD is an independent initiative initially focused on the CHRIST University student ecosystem. It does not claim University affiliation, endorsement or authorisation.

Is online registration and automated matching live today? +

Not yet. This page introduces the MELD model. Its first stage is being developed around assisted, relationship-led introductions; self-service registration, dashboards and automated matching are not currently live.
